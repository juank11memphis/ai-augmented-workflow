# Synthetic Scrum Authoring BRD

This tracked fixture supplies source IDs for the sample Epic and Stories. It is not a product BRD.

- **REQ-01** — An Epic has one coherent outcome and a completion condition.
- **REQ-02** — A Story is one independently reviewable, deployable increment.
- **REQ-03** — A Story uses the required section order.
- **REQ-04** — Acceptance criteria describe observable outcomes.
- **REQ-05** — Epic and Story claims trace to source requirement IDs.
- **REQ-06** — Consider a feature flag only for concrete unfinished-behavior exposure.
- **REQ-07** — Flagged work defines flag-off preservation and flag-on behavior.
- **REQ-08** — A flagged Epic includes final removal and a no-reference check.
- **REQ-09** — Verification distinguishes executable checks from human judgment.
- **REQ-10** — Authoring guidance remains scoped and distributed through templates.
